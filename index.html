How it works

No long lectures. No guessing what to do next. Every skill follows the same clear shape.

1

Pick a skill, learn one thing a day

Choose from 123 skills and follow one focused lesson a day. Each day has a real video, practical steps and a hands-on task that builds on the day before.

2

Prove it with a quiz and a task

Every day ends with a hands-on task and a short quiz. Pass the quiz at 70 percent or more to complete the day. You can retry as many times as you need.

3

Finish the capstone, get certified

Build, make or perform your capstone in the final week. When all 30 days are complete and every capstone check is ticked, your certificate unlocks with a verification code employers can check.

Your certificate

A printable certificate with your name, the skill, the issue date, and a verification code in the format SC-YYYY-XXXXXX. Anyone can check it on the verification page.
