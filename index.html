How it works

No long lectures. No guessing what to do next. Every day has the same clear shape.

1

Learn one thing a day

Watch the day's video and work through the practical steps. Each lesson builds on the one before it, from your first web page to a deployed full-stack app.

2

Prove it with a quiz and a task

Every day ends with a hands-on task and a 3 question quiz. Pass the quiz at 70 percent or more to complete the day. You can retry as many times as you need.

3

Finish the capstone, get certified

Build your own full-stack app in the final week. When all 30 days are complete and every capstone check is ticked, your certificate unlocks with a verification code employers can check.
