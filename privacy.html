Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you learn, we also collect your learning progress, quiz results and certificates.

How sign-in works

Sign-in runs through Supabase and Google. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us so we can create your account. We never see your Google password.

How we use your information

Your progress and certificates are stored so the course can keep your place, mark days complete and issue your certificate. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Certificate verification

Anyone with a certificate verification code can check the learner name, skill and issue date on the verification page. That check is public by design, so employers can confirm a certificate is real.
